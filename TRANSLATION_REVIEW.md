## 5. Reviewer Sign-Off Checklist

- [ ] **Tamil Medical Terminology Validation**: Verify that clinical descriptions do not sound overly archaic while remaining dignified and unambiguous.
- [ ] **Hindi Devanagari Dialect Suitability**: Verify that Hindi wording is comprehensible to both urban and semi-rural users without overly Sanskritized or localized slang.
- [ ] **Emergency Contact Numbers**: Confirm that **112** (National Emergency), **108** (Ambulance), and **14416** (Tele-MANAS Mental Health Helpline) are prominently visible and unambiguous in both scripts.
- [ ] **Visual Layout & Truncation**: Confirm that buttons and navigation labels with longer Tamil/Hindi compound words (e.g. `விருப்பத்தேர்வுகள்`, `ஆலோசனை வரலாறு`, `आपातकालीन सहायता`) wrap gracefully on mobile viewports (320px–390px).
